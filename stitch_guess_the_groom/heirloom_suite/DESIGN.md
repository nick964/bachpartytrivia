STYLE: Modern heirloom wedding stationery — elegant, playful, premium, like a letterpress invitation suite come to life, NOT a tech dashboard. Palette: dusty french blue (#5E7FA6) and soft sky blue on warm ivory (#FBF9F4), champagne-gold accent used sparingly for celebratory/premium moments, deep slate blue for text (no black or gray). Background texture: subtle dotted-swiss pin-dots on ivory. Cards: double-keyline thin borders, scalloped top edges on hero cards, letterpress-style inset shadow (no drop shadows). Buttons: soft rectangles with slightly rounded corners, dusty blue fill, ivory text. Typography: high-contrast refined serif for headlines in title case with italic for emphasis words ("What did the groom *say?*"); clean geometric sans-serif for body and UI; no script fonts. Decorative accents in fine-line single-color engraved-illustration style: sparkle clusters, ribbon/bow, intertwined thin-line rings, champagne coupe, bunting garlands. Logo mark: a small wax-seal-style circular monogram badge. Airy, generous ivory whitespace throughout. Mobile-first for guest-facing screens.
